Welcome to Gabsweb CashFlow

Gabsweb CashFlow helps you manage your money, understand your spending and plan your finances in one place.

You can track your accounts and transactions, plan how to use your income, monitor savings, manage financial goals and review your financial activity.

This guide explains how each part of the application works and how the different features connect.

1. Dashboard

Your Dashboard gives you a quick overview of your financial position.

You can see:

  • Your total account balance.
  • Your available balance after current savings.
  • Your income, expenses and net cash flow.
  • Your financial health indicators.
  • Your income and expense trends.
  • Recent transactions.
  • Budget, Savings and Goals summaries.
  • Your Planner summary.

The dashboard uses your recorded financial information. If required information cannot be loaded, the affected figures may be shown as unavailable instead of displaying guessed values.

Understanding available balance

Available balance is calculated by subtracting your current Savings balance from your total account balance.

Understanding the cash-flow indicator

The dashboard's displayed rate is based on income minus expenses, divided by income. It represents net cash flow as a proportion of income; it is not a measure of Budget Savings allocations.

2. My Finances

My Finances is where you record and manage your actual money.

Accounts

Create accounts for the places where you keep your money, including:

  • Bank accounts.
  • Cash wallets.
  • Savings accounts.
  • Mobile money.
  • Investments.
  • Other account types.

Each account can have an opening balance, opening-balance date and description.

You can edit and archive accounts. Archiving keeps their financial history available.

An account with transaction history cannot be deleted. Archive it instead.

Transactions

Record three types of transactions:

  • Income: Money received.
  • Expense: Money spent.
  • Transfer: Money moved between two of your accounts.

Transfers require two different accounts belonging to you.

Transactions can include an amount, date, description, category, account and optional notes.

Expenses can also be linked to a Budget allocation or a Goal where appropriate.

An expense linked to a Budget allocation must fall within that allocation's financial month.

Your account balances are calculated from opening balances and recorded transactions. Budget allocations and Goal assignments do not directly change account balances.

3. Budget & Money Allocation

Budget helps you plan how to distribute your monthly income.

You can create allocations for categories such as Feeding, Transport, Family Support, Savings and Goals.

Each allocation can have a name, planned amount, month, description and status.

How Budget works

Your monthly income comes from actual income transactions recorded in My Finances.

Allocations are financial plans. Creating an allocation does not mean that money has been spent, saved or transferred.

When you record an expense, you can link it to the relevant allocation. The expense then counts towards that allocation's actual spending.

Expenses that are not linked to an allocation still count as real expenses in My Finances.

You can spend more than an allocation's planned amount. The application tracks the excess so you can see where you have overspent.

Understanding your Budget figures

Monthly income
Income transactions recorded during the month.
Total allocated
The amount assigned across your Budget allocations.
Unallocated funds
Income that has not been allocated. This figure never displays as a negative amount.
Over-allocated funds
The amount by which your allocations exceed your monthly income.
Actual spending
Expenses linked to Budget allocations.
Remaining
The amount left against your allocations after linked spending.

Over-allocation and overspending are different. Over-allocation means you planned more than your income. Overspending means actual expenses exceeded an allocation.

4. Savings

Savings shows the money allocated to Savings through your Budget.

You do not create separate savings contributions. Your Savings amount is calculated from non-cancelled Budget allocations in the Savings category.

The page shows your monthly Savings allocations, cumulative Savings balance, withdrawals and Savings history.

Recording a withdrawal

When you take money out of Savings, record a withdrawal with:

  • Amount.
  • Date.
  • Reason.

A withdrawal reduces your available Savings balance.

You cannot withdraw more than your available Savings.

Important: Recording a withdrawal is a Savings record only. It does not automatically create an expense, transfer money between accounts or change an account balance. If you actually spent the money, record the expense separately in My Finances.

5. Goals

Goals helps you plan and fund future purchases or financial targets.

Create a Goal with a name, target amount, optional target date and description.

Funding your Goals

Your Goals funding comes from non-cancelled Budget allocations in the Goals category.

You can assign available Goals funding to individual Goals.

Assigning money to a Goal is a planning action. It does not create income, an expense, a transfer or an account-balance change.

You can reverse an assignment to release the money back into the available Goals funding pool.

Completing a Goal

A Goal becomes fully funded when its assigned funding reaches its target amount.

A fully funded Goal does not automatically mean that you have purchased the item.

To record the purchase, create an expense in My Finances and link it to the relevant Goal.

The linked expense records the purchase and updates the Goal's funding position.

6. Planner

Planner helps you organise your daily activities and projects.

Daily planning

You can create daily plans containing:

  • Your Top Three priorities.
  • Tasks.
  • Appointments and meetings.
  • Notes and remarks.

You can move between dates and review your daily plans.

Projects

Create projects to organise larger activities.

Projects support details, dates, status, milestones and progress tracking.

Planner activities are separate from your financial records. Creating a project or milestone does not automatically create a transaction or affect your account balances.

7. Work Schedule

Work Schedule lets you organise and review your work shifts.

You can view your schedule in monthly, weekly and list formats.

Manual schedule entries

Users can create and manage their own manual schedule entries.

Automatic work rotations

Automatic rotation generation and its related settings are available only to users with approved GCTN or Apex Pay workplace access.

Users with General or Pending status can use manual schedule entries but cannot access approved workplace rotation features.

If you need access to those workplace features, contact the platform owner for approval.

8. Reports & Insights

Reports & Insights helps you understand your financial activity over time.

You can view monthly and yearly reports.

Reports include:

  • Financial overview.
  • Cash-flow trends.
  • Income and expense categories.
  • Transfers.
  • Budget allocation performance.
  • Savings analysis.
  • Goal progress and purchases.
  • Period comparisons.
  • Useful financial insights.

You can export a report as a PDF.

Reports are read-only. They use the same financial records and calculations as the rest of the application. They do not create or modify transactions, allocations, Savings or Goals.

9. Settings

Settings lets you manage your personal and application preferences.

Available sections include:

  • Personal profile: Your personal details, email, professional role and profile image.
  • Password and Google sign-in: Manage your Gabsweb password and Google sign-in connection.
  • Financial preferences: Review your currency and financial-month settings.
  • Work preferences: Manage available personal work settings and details.
  • Appearance: Choose between light and dark themes.

Your financial currency is NGN, and financial periods follow the calendar month.

10. Understanding how your finances connect

It is important to understand the difference between actual financial records and financial plans.

Feature What it does
My Finances Records actual income, expenses and transfers.
Budget Plans how monthly income should be allocated.
Savings Shows Budget Savings allocations and records withdrawals.
Goals Organises funding from Budget Goals allocations towards planned purchases.
Planner Organises daily activities and projects.
Reports & Insights Summarises existing financial records and calculations.

Remember

  • Only recorded transactions change account balances.
  • Budget allocations do not create transactions.
  • Savings allocations do not automatically move money into another account.
  • Savings withdrawals are not automatically expenses or transfers.
  • Assigning money to a Goal does not change account balances.
  • A Goal purchase must be recorded as an expense in My Finances.
  • Reports display financial information; they do not change it.
